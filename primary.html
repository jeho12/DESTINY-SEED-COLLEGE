Foundation Stages

A Balanced, World-Class Primary Curriculum

Blending the British Cambridge Primary Framework with Nigerian National Curriculum benchmarks to ensure seamless progression into secondary education.

Ages 2 – 5 Years

Early Years (EYFS: Creche & Nursery)

Sensory play, communication development, gross and fine motor skills, Jolly Phonics sounds, numbers, and social harmony in a colorful, nurturing sanctuary.

Ages 6 – 8 Years

Lower Primary (Grades 1 – 3)

Building strong reading comprehension, creative writing, mastery of mental arithmetic, scientific inquiry, cultural appreciation, and foundational computer literacy.

Ages 9 – 11 Years

Upper Primary (Grades 4 – 6)

Advanced critical thinking, Common Entrance & National Examination preparation, coding with Scratch, public speaking, leadership roles, and sportsmanship.
