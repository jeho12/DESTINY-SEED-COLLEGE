Strategic Web Architecture

One Institution. One Central Domain.
Infinite Possibilities.

Instead of scattering the school’s digital presence across two disconnected websites, Destiny Seeds operates under a unified domain (destinyseeds.edu.ng). This delivers a formidable online brand, reduces maintenance costs, and makes parent access effortless.

Single Brand Authority: Builds stronger prestige and Google search rankings.
50% Cost Savings: One web hosting, security certificate, and maintenance plan.
Parent Convenience: One parent portal to monitor both primary & college wards.
Dedicated Sections: Distinct sub-pages preserve each school's unique character.
