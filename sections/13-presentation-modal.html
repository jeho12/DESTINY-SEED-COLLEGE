Why A Unified Single Domain is Best for Destiny Seeds

Use these talking points during your presentation to demonstrate the strategic advantages of hosting both Primary and College under one unified domain (e.g. destinyseeds.edu.ng):

1. One Unified Brand Identity

Destiny Seeds is known by parents as one institution. Having two separate domains confuses prospective parents. A single domain builds a formidable, prestigious digital presence.

2. Significant Cost & Maintenance Savings

Operating two distinct websites doubles annual hosting fees, SSL security certificates, and technical webmaster maintenance costs. A single domain cuts costs by over 50%.

3. Maximum Google Search Ranking (SEO)

Google consolidates backlinks, traffic, and credibility onto one powerful domain. Every achievement from the college boosts the primary school's visibility, and vice-versa.

4. Frictionless Parent Experience & Retention

Parents with children in both wings only need one website and portal to check calendars, pay fees, and track academic reports, naturally encouraging primary pupils to transition into the college wing.

Suggested Domain Names: destinyseeds.edu.ng (Most prestigious for accredited educational institutions) or destinyseedschools.com.
